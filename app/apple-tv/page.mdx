# Apple TV troubleshooting hub

Welcome to the Apple TV fix-it hub. Start with the quick wins below, then dive into the focused guides for HDMI, Atmos, lip-sync, Wi‑Fi, and app-specific issues like Netflix, Disney+, and YouTube. Every article follows real-world triage order so you can restore picture and sound fast.

## Quick fixes most people should try first

1. Connect Apple TV to the TV’s best HDMI/eARC port with a certified cable.
2. Set Apple TV to 4K SDR with **Match Dynamic Range** and **Match Frame Rate** ON.
3. Enable eARC + pass-through on the TV if you use a soundbar or receiver.
4. Use Ethernet or strong 5 GHz/Wi‑Fi 6 when possible; avoid VPNs during testing.
