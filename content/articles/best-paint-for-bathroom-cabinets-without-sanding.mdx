## What to Look for in Paint for Bathroom Cabinets Without Sanding

Choosing the right paint for bathroom cabinets when you want to skip sanding requires a product specifically engineered for adhesion and durability on challenging surfaces. The key factors revolve around how well the paint bonds to existing finishes, its resilience in a humid environment, and its overall longevity. Look for paints that explicitly state "no-sanding required" or "self-priming" on their labels, as these formulations contain strong bonding agents designed to grip smooth, non-porous surfaces like laminate, thermofoil, or previously painted wood without the need for abrasive prep work.

Beyond adhesion, durability is paramount for bathroom cabinets. These surfaces endure daily contact, splashes, and the constant presence of moisture. A high-quality paint will offer excellent resistance to chipping, scratching, and peeling. Many top-performing options are urethane-modified acrylics or enamels, which provide a hard, protective shell. Additionally, given the bathroom's inherent humidity, mildew and mold resistance are critical. Paints formulated with mildewcides help prevent fungal growth, maintaining a cleaner, healthier appearance over time. Finally, consider the finish: semi-gloss or satin are popular choices for cabinets because they are easy to clean and offer good durability, while a flat finish can be harder to maintain in a high-traffic area.
