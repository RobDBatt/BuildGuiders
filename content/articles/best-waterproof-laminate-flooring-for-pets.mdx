## What to Look for in Waterproof Laminate Flooring for Pets

Choosing the right waterproof laminate flooring for a home with pets requires focusing on specific features that address common challenges like accidents, scratches, and everyday wear. While traditional laminate is susceptible to water damage, modern waterproof laminate options incorporate advanced technology to protect against spills and pet messes, making them a viable choice.

Key considerations include the flooring's AC (Abrasion Class) rating, which indicates its durability against wear and tear. For homes with active pets, an AC4 or AC5 rating is generally recommended to withstand claws and heavy foot traffic. The core material is also crucial; look for high-density fiberboard (HDF) cores treated for water resistance, or newer rigid core constructions that offer superior dimensional stability and waterproof properties. The locking mechanism between planks is equally important, as a tight, secure seal prevents moisture from seeping into the subfloor.

Beyond water resistance and durability, consider the surface texture. A slightly textured or embossed surface can provide better traction for pets, reducing the risk of slips and falls, especially for older animals. Smooth, highly glossy finishes, while attractive, can be slick underfoot. Finally, ease of cleaning is paramount. A flooring surface that resists stains and can be easily wiped clean will save you time and effort in managing pet-related messes.

## What to Skip

When selecting waterproof laminate for pets, avoid any laminate product that does not explicitly state "waterproof" or "highly water-resistant" with a clear warranty against pet accidents. Traditional laminate, even if it has a durable wear layer, will swell and warp if exposed to moisture for too long, making it unsuitable for pet homes. Also, steer clear of laminate with an AC3 rating or lower if you have large or very active pets, as it may not hold up to their claws and play. Finally, extremely smooth or high-gloss finishes, while visually appealing, can create a slippery surface for pets, increasing the risk of injury.
