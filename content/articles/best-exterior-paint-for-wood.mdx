## Best Exterior Paint for Wood in 2026

Wood is the most demanding surface you can paint outside. It swells when it rains, shrinks in August, and cedar or redwood will bleed brown tannin stains straight through a cheap topcoat. So the right question isn't "what's the best exterior paint" in the abstract. It's what's best for the job in front of you: a whole house, a fence, or bare boards that have never seen paint. Here's how to split it.

## What actually matters on wood

Three things separate paint that lasts 12 years on wood from paint that peels in 4. Flexibility, because a brittle film cracks when boards move. Adhesion, which is mostly about prep and primer rather than the topcoat. And UV-stable pigment, which decides whether your navy blue is still navy in 2031. Sheen matters less than people think; satin and low-lustre both perform well on siding, while flat hides surface flaws on rough boards.

Whatever you choose, the math stays the same: exterior paint covers roughly 250 to 350 square feet per gallon per coat on smooth wood, and meaningfully less on rough-sawn boards that drink paint.

## Repainting a whole house

This is where spending up makes sense, because labor dwarfs material cost. Sherwin-Williams Emerald is the safest all-around pick: self-priming over sound existing paint, very good peel resistance, and rated for application down to 35°F, so you can paint in April and October without gambling. Benjamin Moore Regal Select is the value play in the premium tier. It breathes well on older wood and costs noticeably less than Aura while behaving almost as well.

If your house is older with weathered, checked boards, Sherwin-Williams Duration is the specialist. Its thicker high-build film bridges hairline cracks that thinner paints telegraph straight through. That same thickness means it's less forgiving to brush, so keep a wet edge and don't overwork it.

## Dark or bold colors

Buy Benjamin Moore Aura and don't overthink it. Deep colors fade fastest because they absorb the most UV, and Aura's Color Lock pigments hold them visibly longer than the competition. On a black or deep-navy farmhouse repaint, the difference by year five is real. For white or beige siding, Aura is still excellent, but Emerald or Regal Select will get you 95% of the result for less money.

## Fences, sheds, and budget jobs

Behr Premium Plus Exterior is the honest budget answer. It's a true 100% acrylic, it's on the shelf at Home Depot today, and on a fence or shed the difference between it and a premium line may never matter, since those surfaces usually get repainted for looks before the paint actually fails. Put your savings into a second coat instead; two coats of Premium Plus will outlast one coat of anything.

## Bare wood: the step everyone skips

New boards, sanded-down spots, and anywhere you can see raw grain need primer first. Acrylic paint straight over bare cedar or redwood looks fine for a month, then brown tannin blotches ghost through. Zinsser Cover Stain, an oil-based primer, seals that bleed and gives chalky old boards real grip. It recoats in about 2 hours, so primer and first topcoat can happen the same day. Spot-prime bare patches on repaints; full-prime anything that's all raw wood.

## Timing the job

Paint chemistry only works inside its window. Most acrylics want surface temperatures between 50°F and 90°F and no rain for 24 hours after application, and a board that's hot to the touch in direct afternoon sun will flash-dry the paint and leave lap marks. Follow the shade around the house instead of the clock. June mornings are close to ideal in most of the country, which is exactly why painters book out now.

## What to actually buy

Most wood-sided houses: Emerald in satin, with Cover Stain on any bare spots. Dark colors get Aura. An older house with cracking boards wants Duration. And the fence gets Behr Premium Plus, two coats. Pick one and move on to the part that actually determines how long the job lasts, which is prep.

Before you order, measure. Gallon math on a whole house is easy to botch, and a short trip back to the store mid-job with a custom color is a recipe for a visible color shift between batches.
