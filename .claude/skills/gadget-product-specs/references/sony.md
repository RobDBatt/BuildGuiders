## HDR Format Support

Sony supports: **HDR10, Dolby Vision, HLG**

Sony does NOT support: **HDR10+** on most models (limited to select models with Google TV and specific regions)

## HDMI Port Configuration (A80L / A95L / X95L typical)

**Critical rule for Sony:** eARC is typically on **HDMI 3**, but high-bandwidth (4K@120) ports are HDMI 1 and HDMI 2. This means your gaming console (HDMI 1 or 2) and your soundbar/AVR (HDMI 3 eARC) are on DIFFERENT ports — this is the intended setup.

**A80L (2023):**
- HDMI 1: HDMI 2.1 (4K@120, 48Gbps)
- HDMI 2: HDMI 2.1 (4K@120, 48Gbps)
- HDMI 3: HDMI 2.0 + **eARC** (4K@60 only from this port)
- HDMI 4: HDMI 2.0

**X95L (2023):**
- HDMI 1, 2: HDMI 2.1 (4K@120)
- HDMI 3: eARC + HDMI 2.0
- HDMI 4: HDMI 2.0

**X90L (2023):**
- HDMI 1: HDMI 2.1 (4K@120)
- HDMI 2, 3, 4: HDMI 2.0 (HDMI 3 has ARC — but check whether it supports eARC on specific builds)

Always state port numbers explicitly per article. Don't generalize.

## Smart Platform

- **Google TV** (on 2021 and later models)
- Older models (2020 and before) ran **Android TV**
- **CEC name: Bravia Sync** (NOT Anynet+, NOT SimpLink)

## VRR Support

- HDMI Forum VRR added via firmware on A80K/A90K and X9x series (2022+)
- VRR and HDMI 2.1 are restricted to HDMI 1 and 2 — not HDMI 3 or 4

## Audio

- Dolby Atmos decoded internally on XR models
- eARC on HDMI 3 supports lossless TrueHD passthrough to AVR
- Sony's Acoustic Surface Audio (on OLED models) uses the screen itself as a speaker

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Never say Sony supports HDR10+.** Sony's HDR formats are HDR10, Dolby Vision, HLG.
2. **Never say eARC is on HDMI 1 or HDMI 2 for Sony.** eARC is on HDMI 3 on most 2022+ Sony TVs.
3. **Never say "Anynet+" or "SimpLink" for Sony.** Sony = **Bravia Sync**.
4. **Do not claim 4K@120 on HDMI 3 for Sony.** HDMI 3 on Sony is typically for eARC but runs at 2.0 speeds (4K@60 max). Game consoles should go to HDMI 1 or 2.
5. **QD-OLED and W-OLED are different panel types.** A95L uses QD-OLED; A80L uses W-OLED.
