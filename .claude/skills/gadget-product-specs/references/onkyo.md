# Onkyo Reference

## Brand Context

Onkyo was acquired by VOXX International (which also owns Integra) after the original Onkyo corporation entered bankruptcy in 2022. The Onkyo brand continues under VOXX. Onkyo and Integra share platforms.

## Multi-Room Ecosystem

**Onkyo does NOT have its own multi-room platform like HEOS or MusicCast.** Onkyo receivers use:
- **DTS Play-Fi** on some models (multiroom over Wi-Fi)
- **Chromecast Built-in** on some models
- **AirPlay 2** on select models

Do NOT write that Onkyo uses HEOS (that's Denon/Marantz) or MusicCast (that's Yamaha).

## HDMI Passthrough Capabilities

| Model | 4K@60Hz | 4K@120Hz | eARC |
|-------|--------|---------|------|
| TX-NR6100 | All inputs | 2× HDMI 2.1 ports | HDMI OUT main |
| TX-NR5100 | All inputs | Limited or none | HDMI OUT main |
| TX-NR4100 | All inputs | 1× HDMI 2.1 | HDMI OUT main |

**Port asymmetry: Not all HDMI inputs are 2.1.** Only the TX-NR6100 has 2 HDMI 2.1 ports. Lower models may have 1 or no 4K@120Hz input ports.

## eARC Support

- **TX-NR6100:** eARC on HDMI Main Monitor Out — confirmed
- **TX-NR5100 and below:** Verify per model; eARC support is not guaranteed on all Onkyo models
- **Older pre-2022 Onkyo models** (TX-NR696, TX-NR797, etc.) were ARC only, not eARC

## Dolby Atmos & DTS:X

- TX-NR6100, TX-NR5100: Dolby Atmos + DTS:X supported
- **AccuEQ room correction:** Onkyo's proprietary room correction system (not Audyssey, not Dirac)
- Auro-3D: NOT included standard on Onkyo (unlike some Denon/Marantz models)

## Wireless Protocols

- **Wi-Fi:** Built-in (Wi-Fi 5 on NR6100)
- **Bluetooth:** A2DP for streaming from phone
- **AirPlay 2:** TX-NR6100 (verify per update)
- **FlareConnect:** Onkyo/Pioneer multi-room protocol (legacy, not widely used)

## Historical Note

Pre-bankruptcy Onkyo had popular models like TX-NR696, TX-RZ50, etc. The VOXX-era Onkyo (post-2022) has a smaller lineup. Many troubleshooting articles may reference older SKUs that are no longer in production. Still worth covering for support articles.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Never write "Onkyo HEOS" or "Onkyo MusicCast."** Onkyo uses DTS Play-Fi or Chromecast Built-in.
2. **Do not claim Onkyo supports Auro-3D standard.** Not included without optional upgrade (if available).
3. **Do not assume eARC on older Onkyo models.** Pre-2020 models were ARC only.
4. **Do not confuse Onkyo and Integra.** Integra is a higher-end sibling brand (same VOXX parent), not identical models.
5. **Never say Onkyo is Japanese-owned.** Post-2022 Onkyo is owned by VOXX International (US company).
