# Marantz Reference

## Brand Context

Marantz and Denon share the same parent company (Sound United, now Harman). They use the **same HEOS multi-room platform** and often share amplifier topology and DSP architecture, but Marantz targets audiophile/home theater aesthetics.

## Multi-Room Ecosystem

**Marantz uses HEOS, NOT MusicCast.** HEOS is the Denon/Marantz multi-room platform. Do NOT write "MusicCast" for Marantz — MusicCast is Yamaha's platform.

## HDMI Passthrough Capabilities

| Model | 4K@60Hz | 4K@120Hz |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Cinema 60 | All ports | 2× HDMI 2.1 ports (4K@120Hz) |
| Cinema 70s | All ports | 1–2× HDMI 2.1 ports |

**Port asymmetry applies.** Not all HDMI inputs pass 4K@120Hz. Typically 1–2 ports are HDMI 2.1 (for gaming/Apple TV 4K), rest are HDMI 2.0.

## eARC Support

- **Cinema 60, Cinema 50, Cinema 70s:** eARC on HDMI OUT (main monitor output)
- eARC is the correct spec for lossless Dolby Atmos TrueHD from modern TVs
- Older Marantz models (SR-series, pre-2021) had ARC only

## Dolby Atmos & DTS:X

- All Cinema-series models support **Dolby Atmos** (height channels)
- All Cinema-series models support **DTS:X** (object-based DTS)
- **Auro-3D:** Available on Cinema 60 and above (optional license or built-in)
- **IMAX Enhanced:** Available on select Cinema models

## Room Correction

- **Audyssey MultEQ XT32:** Used on Cinema 60 and above (same as Denon)
- **Dirac Live:** Available as optional paid upgrade on some Cinema models
- Marantz targets audiophile users — often pairs with high-quality separate components

## Wireless Protocols

- **Wi-Fi:** Built-in (used for HEOS, AirPlay 2, Bluetooth A2DP)
- **AirPlay 2:** Supported on all Cinema-series
- **HEOS:** Supports streaming from Spotify, Tidal, TuneIn, local network
- **Bluetooth:** For headphone/speaker output (not main listening path)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Never write "MusicCast" for Marantz.** Marantz uses HEOS (same as Denon).
2. **Never write "Yamaha HEOS receiver."** HEOS is Denon/Marantz only.
3. **Do not claim all HDMI ports are 2.1.** Only 1–2 ports per model are HDMI 2.1.
4. **Marantz is a pre-amp + power amp architecture at high end.** The AMP10/AV10 are separates, not integrated AVRs.
5. **Do not confuse Cinema-series SKUs with old SR-/NR- numbered models.** Marantz rebranded to Cinema-series naming in 2022.
