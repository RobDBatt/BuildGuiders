## Multi-Room Ecosystem

Denon uses **HEOS** for multi-room audio.
- HEOS is shared with Marantz (same parent company: Sound United/Masimo)
- Do NOT attribute MusicCast (Yamaha) or Chromecast Built-in (Onkyo) to Denon

## HDMI Configuration

**eARC location:** HDMI Out 1 (the primary zone output) on all Denon receivers.

**HDMI 2.1 inputs:** Varies by model. AVR-X3800H and up have HDMI 2.1 on ALL inputs. AVR-X2800H / AVR-S760H have HDMI 2.1 on 2 inputs only.

**Passthrough spec:** Check per model — not all HDMI 2.1-capable receivers pass 4K@120Hz through reliably on early firmware.

## Audio Format Support by Tier

**X3800H and above:** Dolby Atmos, Dolby Atmos Height Virtualization, DTS:X, DTS:X Pro, Auro-3D, IMAX Enhanced

**S760H / X2800H:** Dolby Atmos, DTS:X (no Auro-3D on budget models)

**S660H:** Dolby Atmos, DTS:X (HDMI 2.0 only — no 4K@120 support)

## Audyssey Room Correction

- MultEQ XT32: AVR-X3800H, X4800H, X6800H
- MultEQ XT: AVR-X2800H, S970H
- MultEQ: S760H and below

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Never attribute MusicCast to Denon.** Denon uses **HEOS**. Yamaha uses MusicCast.
2. **Never claim all Denon receivers have HDMI 2.1 on all inputs.** Only the X3800H and above have HDMI 2.1 across all inputs.
3. **Never say eARC is on HDMI Out 2.** eARC is always HDMI Out 1 on Denon.
4. **Do not confuse Denon and Marantz as the same brand.** They share HEOS and a parent company but are separate product lines with different pricing and aesthetics.
5. **Never quote wattage without noting the test conditions** (8Ω, 2-channel driven, 0.05% THD is the industry-standard spec condition).
