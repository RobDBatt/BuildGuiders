# Vizio Reference

## Models In Scope

| Series | Panel Type | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| P-Series Quantum X | QLED | 4K, 120Hz, HDR10+, Dolby Vision, SmartCast |
| P-Series Quantum | QLED | 4K, 120Hz, HDR10+, Dolby Vision, SmartCast |
| M-Series Quantum | QLED | 4K, 60Hz (some 120Hz), HDR10+, Dolby Vision |
| V-Series | LCD | 4K, 60Hz, HDR10, limited smart features |
| OLED (discontinued) | OLED | Limited production run, discontinued |

## HDR Format Support

- **Dolby Vision:** YES — Vizio supports Dolby Vision on P-Series and M-Series
- **HDR10+:** YES — Vizio supports HDR10+ on P-Series (unusual — one of few non-Samsung brands to support both)
- **HDR10:** YES (universal)
- **HLG:** YES (broadcast)

**Vizio is unusual: supports BOTH Dolby Vision AND HDR10+** on high-end models. This is an exception to the general "brand supports one or the other" rule.

## Smart TV Platform

- **SmartCast:** Vizio's proprietary smart TV platform (Chromecast Built-in based)
- No traditional app store like Roku or Google TV
- App catalog is limited vs. Roku/Google TV
- **Chromecast Built-in:** Every SmartCast TV has Chromecast capability

## HDMI Configuration (P-Series Quantum X example)

- **Ports 1–3:** HDMI 2.0 (4K@60Hz)
- **Port 5 (eARC):** HDMI 2.1 (eARC capable)
- Some P-Series revisions have 1× HDMI 2.1 for gaming
- **Always check which ports support 4K@120Hz** — P-Series gaming port varies by revision

## Panel Type Clarity

- **P-Series / M-Series Quantum:** QLED = quantum dot LCD (NOT OLED)
- The discontinued Vizio OLED was a genuine OLED panel but is no longer in production
- Most current Vizio models are QLED (LCD-based)

## CEC Features

- **CEC name:** HDMI CEC — no proprietary brand name for Vizio
- Vizio uses standard CEC; no marketing name like Anynet+ or SimpLink

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Do not say Vizio doesn't support HDR10+.** P-Series supports both Dolby Vision AND HDR10+.
2. **Do not say SmartCast has a full app store.** It's Chromecast-based, limited app selection.
3. **Do not call current Vizio TVs OLED.** The OLED line was discontinued.
4. **Do not assume all HDMI ports are 2.1.** Typically only 1 port (the dedicated gaming/eARC port) is 2.1.
5. **Check HDR support by model tier.** V-Series has very limited HDR; P-Series has full HDR suite.
