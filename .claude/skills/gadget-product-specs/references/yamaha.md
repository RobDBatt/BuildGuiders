## Multi-Room Ecosystem

Yamaha uses **MusicCast** for multi-room audio.
- MusicCast is Yamaha's proprietary ecosystem — it is NOT HEOS (Denon/Marantz)
- Do NOT attribute HEOS to Yamaha receivers
- MusicCast works via Wi-Fi; the MusicCast Controller app controls zones

## HDMI Configuration

**eARC location:** HDMI Out 1 (Main Zone output) on all Yamaha receivers with eARC.

**HDMI 2.1 availability:**
- AVENTAGE A6A and above: HDMI 2.1 on ALL inputs (4K@120, 8K support)
- RX-V6A: HDMI 2.1 on inputs 1 and 2 only; HDMI 2.0 on inputs 3–7
- RX-V4A and below: HDMI 2.0 only — no 4K@120

**8K passthrough:** AVENTAGE A6A and above only.

## Audio Formats by Tier

**AVENTAGE A6A and above:** Dolby Atmos, DTS:X, Auro-3D, IMAX Enhanced
**AVENTAGE A4A / RX-V6A:** Dolby Atmos, DTS:X (no Auro-3D)
**RX-V4A and below:** Dolby Atmos, DTS:X (HDMI 2.0 only)

## Room Correction

- YPAO (Yamaha Parametric room Acoustic Optimizer): all models
- YPAO RSC (Reflected Sound Control): AVENTAGE series
- YPAO Volume: most models (automated EQ at low listening levels)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Never attribute HEOS to Yamaha.** Yamaha uses **MusicCast**. HEOS is Denon/Marantz.
2. **Never claim all Yamaha receivers support HDMI 2.1.** Only A4A and above in the AVENTAGE line; the budget V-series has HDMI 2.1 only on specific inputs (V6A) or not at all (V4A, V2A).
3. **Never say eARC is on HDMI Out 2.** eARC is on HDMI Out 1 on Yamaha receivers.
4. **Do not confuse AVENTAGE and standard RX-V.** AVENTAGE has dedicated anti-vibration feet and typically better room correction and more audio format support.
5. **Auro-3D is only in AVENTAGE series.** RX-V series does not decode Auro-3D.
