# Samsung TV Reference

## CRITICAL RULE

**Samsung TVs do NOT support Dolby Vision.** This is a deliberate business decision by Samsung — they co-developed **HDR10+** as a competing open standard and do not license Dolby Vision. Any article claiming Samsung + Dolby Vision is immediately false. This applies to ALL Samsung TV models, including OLED (QD-OLED), Neo QLED, QLED, and Crystal UHD.

## HDR Format Support

Samsung supports: **HDR10, HDR10+, HLG**

Samsung does NOT support: **Dolby Vision** (on any model, ever)

**HDR10+ vs Dolby Vision:**
- Both are "dynamic HDR" formats that adjust metadata scene-by-scene
- HDR10+ is open standard (Samsung/Amazon); Dolby Vision is licensed (Dolby)
- Most streaming services offer content in both, but platform support varies

## HDMI Port Configuration

**Important:** Samsung port numbers are assigned right-to-left on the back panel on most models. HDMI 1 is typically the eARC port AND an HDMI 2.1 port.

**S90C / S95C (QD-OLED, 2023):**
- HDMI 1: HDMI 2.1 (4K@120, eARC)
- HDMI 2: HDMI 2.1 (4K@120)
- HDMI 3: HDMI 2.0
- HDMI 4: HDMI 2.0

**QN90C / QN95C (Neo QLED, 2023):**
- HDMI 1: HDMI 2.1 (4K@120, eARC)
- HDMI 4: HDMI 2.1 (4K@120)
- HDMI 2, 3: HDMI 2.0

**QN85C and lower:** Typically only HDMI 1 supports 4K@120Hz.

**Crystal UHD:** All ports HDMI 2.0 — no 4K@120Hz, no VRR.

## Smart Platform

- **Tizen OS / Samsung Smart Hub** (not called "Samsung Smart TV OS" — full name is Tizen)
- **CEC name: Anynet+** (NOT SimpLink, NOT Bravia Sync)
- One Connect Box (external connection hub) is present on Frame TV and some QN9x models

## VRR Support

- HDMI Forum VRR + AMD FreeSync Premium Pro on 2022 and later flagship models
- N-series budget models often have only FreeSync, not HDMI Forum VRR

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **NEVER claim Samsung supports Dolby Vision.** Replace with HDR10+.
2. **NEVER say "all 4 HDMI ports are 2.1"** — on most Samsung TVs, only 1-2 ports are 2.1.
3. **Never use "SimpLink"** for Samsung — that's LG's name. Samsung = **Anynet+**.
4. **QLED is NOT OLED.** Samsung QLED is LCD + quantum dots. Only QD-OLED (S-series) has an OLED panel.
5. **Do not confuse Neo QLED with QLED.** Neo QLED has Mini-LED backlighting; QLED has standard LED.
